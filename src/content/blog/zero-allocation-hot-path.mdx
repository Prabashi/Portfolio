## Measure first

Start with `[MemoryDiagnoser]` in BenchmarkDotNet and a dotnet-counters trace of `gen-0-gc-count` under load.

## Outline

- Parsing with `ReadOnlySpan<byte>` instead of strings
- Renting buffers from `ArrayPool<T>.Shared`
- `readonly struct` events in a pre-sized ring buffer
- Avoiding closures and boxing in LINQ-free hot loops
